01 About

Research at the intersection of taxation, firms, and policy.

I am the Lars Peter Hansen Associate Professor of Economics and Statistics at Utah State University's Jon M. Huntsman School of Business.

I am a Faculty Research Fellow in the NBER Public Economics Program, an International Research Fellow at the Oxford University Centre for Business Taxation, and a Research Affiliate at CEPR and CESifo.

Since January 2025, I have served as Editor-in-Chief of International Tax and Public Finance. I received my DPhil from the University of Oxford and Nuffield College in 2017.
